Privacy Policy

^kit10^ is a Google Sheets tool. This is what it does and does not do with your data:

  • ^kit10^ only accesses the Google Sheets file it is installed on, and it never opens or reads any other file in your Google Drive.
  • It reads and writes cell content within that same spreadsheet (translations, glossary terms, configuration) to provide its features.
  • It reads your Google Account email address, used only to identify your role (Admin/Translator) within the tool, and this is never shared with anyone or sent anywhere outside your own Google account.
  • All data stays within Google's own infrastructure (Google Sheets, and Apps Script's built-in storage tied to your document). ^kit10^ never sends your data to any external server, never uses analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares data with third parties.
  • Uninstalling the add-on removes its access; your spreadsheet data is untouched.

How sensitive data is protected

^kit10^ has no servers, databases, or infrastructure of its own. All data it works with (translations, glossary entries, project settings, translation-memory cache, and the email addresses used to assign roles within a project) is stored using Google Apps Script's own built-in storage, scoped to the single Google Sheets file the add-on is installed on. That storage is hosted, secured, and access-controlled entirely by Google's own infrastructure, under the same protections as the rest of your Google Account and Google Sheets file.

Access to that data follows the normal sharing permissions of the Google Sheets file itself: only people you've given Editor (or Viewer, where applicable) access to that specific file can open it or run ^kit10^ against it. ^kit10^ does not introduce any separate login, password, or API key of its own, and does not transmit any of this data outside Google's systems. There is no third-party server in the middle.

Data retention and deletion

Data ^kit10^ stores (project configuration, glossary/translation-memory cache, roles, and similar settings) persists for as long as the Google Sheets file it's installed on exists, tied to that file's own lifecycle. It is not kept anywhere separately from that file.

  • You can delete a specific project's configuration at any time from within ^kit10^'s own Settings ("Reset"/"Delete project"), which removes that project's stored data immediately.
  • Deleting the Google Sheets file itself (or removing the add-on's script from it) removes all of ^kit10^'s stored data for that file.
  • You can revoke ^kit10^'s access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately stops any further access. Translated content already saved in your spreadsheet is untouched either way, since it always lived in your own Sheet, not in a separate copy ^kit10^ keeps.
