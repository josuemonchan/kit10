Privacy Policy

^kit10^ is a Google Sheets tool. This is what it does and does not do with your data:

  • ^kit10^ only accesses the Google Sheets file it is installed on — it never opens or reads any other file in your Google Drive.
  • It reads and writes cell content within that same spreadsheet (translations, glossary terms, configuration) to provide its features.
  • It reads your Google Account email address, used only to identify your role (Admin/Translator) within the tool — this is never shared with anyone or sent anywhere outside your own Google account.
  • All data stays within Google's own infrastructure (Google Sheets, and Apps Script's built-in storage tied to your document). ^kit10^ never sends your data to any external server, never uses analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares data with third parties.
  • Uninstalling the add-on removes its access; your spreadsheet data is untouched.
